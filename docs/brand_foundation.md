# PREMIND brand foundation

Verified on 2026-08-08 against the live PREMIND site.

## Sources

- Product site: <https://www.premind.co.kr/>
- Approved wordmark: <https://www.premind.co.kr/brand/wordmark.png>
- Approved square symbol: <https://www.premind.co.kr/icon.png>
- Typeface: <https://github.com/orioncactus/pretendard/releases/tag/v1.3.9>

The bundled wordmark is the 1315 × 341 RGBA source published by PREMIND. Its
only opaque color is `#D25417`; it must not be recolored, stretched, or manually
typeset as replacement text.

Semantic positive, warning, and negative colors remain distinct from the brand
orange. Recording and destructive actions must continue to use negative red.

## Product usage

- Use the official PNG through `AppWordmark`; do not render `PREMIND` as text.
- Use orange for primary actions, selection, progress, and branded emphasis.
- Keep content on warm canvas or white paper with brown-black typography.
- Reserve pill geometry for the strongest action. Use 12–18 px radii elsewhere.
- Prefer spacing, type scale, and warm dividers to grids of elevated cards.
- Use Pretendard 400–700 for product UI; 800 is reserved for short hero copy.
- Native launch screens use brand-soft with the wordmark. App icons use the
  official pencil-P symbol on brand-soft.
