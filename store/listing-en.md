## Full description

PREMIND is a study app that turns a recorded lecture, a video, a YouTube link or a PDF into a transcript, a summary, a mind map and a quiz.

■ One recording, one study pack
Record a class, or upload audio, video, a YouTube link, a PDF or slides. AI writes the transcript (speech to text), a summary you can skim, a mind map of the ideas and practice questions.

■ Read the key points first
Start from the AI summary and tick off what you must remember. Tap any time in the transcript to hear that part again. PDFs and slides turn page by page with a summary for each page.

■ Ask your own material
Ask "Will this be on the exam?" or "Explain overfitting simply". Answers come from your lecture, with the passage they came from.

■ Review with questions, not rereading
Practice with generated questions and flashcards, and see how well you know each lecture this week.

■ Practise talks and interviews out loud
Record a presentation for AI feedback on structure, clarity, evidence and delivery. Answer interview questions aloud and see what to keep and what to fix. Feedback is a practice aid and never predicts a hiring outcome.

■ FAQ
Q. What is PREMIND?
A. A study app where AI turns lecture recordings, videos, YouTube links and PDFs into a transcript, summary, mind map and quiz.
Q. Can it summarise YouTube videos?
A. Paste a public YouTube link and you get a transcript, a summary and questions.
Q. Is it free?
A. The free plan gives 120 minutes a month. Audio and video use a minute per minute; a document uses a minute per page.

AI summaries, questions and feedback can contain mistakes. Check important points against the original.
