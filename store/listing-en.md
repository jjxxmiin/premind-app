## Full description (4000 max, now 3,508)

Stop typing notes. Just press record and listen.
PREMIND writes the transcript, sums up the key points and turns them into practice questions, so you can focus on the class.
PREMIND is an AI study app that turns lecture recordings, YouTube videos and PDFs into a transcript, a summary, a mind map and a quiz.

■ How it works
1. Record or upload: a live lecture, an audio or video file, a YouTube link, a PDF or PowerPoint slides
2. AI organizes it: a full transcript (speech to text), a summary you can skim, a mind map and practice questions
3. Review: test yourself with quizzes and flashcards, and ask your notes anything you're unsure about

■ Lectures in Korean? Study in English
Studying at a Korean university? Set the app to English and record your class as usual. The transcript stays in the language spoken, and your summary, mind map, quiz and answers come out in English.

■ AI lecture recorder and note taker
Record classes, talks and seminars and get a transcript and summary automatically.
Tap any line in the transcript to replay that moment.
Recording keeps going with the screen off, and the original is saved on your phone first.
Live captions show what's being said as you record.

■ Summarize YouTube lectures
Paste a public YouTube link and get a transcript, a summary and questions.
Skim a long lecture video for the key points in minutes.

■ PDF and slide summaries, page by page
Upload a PDF or PowerPoint and get a short summary for every page.
Flip through pages like a book and read the notes side by side.

■ Key points first, then the details
Start with the AI summary and check off what you need to remember.
A mind map shows how the ideas connect. Tap a topic to go one level deeper.

■ Quizzes and flashcards from your own material
Practice questions are made from your lecture, not a generic question bank.
Flashcards help you memorize the key terms, and your progress shows which lecture to review next.

■ Ask your notes
"Will this be on the exam?" "Explain overfitting simply."
Answers come from your own lecture, with the passage they're based on.

■ Practice presentations and interviews
Record a practice talk and get AI feedback on structure, clarity, evidence and delivery.
Answer interview questions out loud and see what to keep and what to fix.
Feedback is a practice aid and never predicts a hiring decision.

■ Great for
University students taking lecture notes, international students in Korea, online course learners, exam and certification prep, and anyone who learns from talks and videos.

■ FAQ
Q. What is PREMIND?
A. An AI study app that turns lecture recordings, videos, YouTube links and PDFs into a transcript, summary, mind map and quiz.
Q. Can it summarize YouTube videos?
A. Yes. Paste a public YouTube link and you'll get a transcript, a summary and questions.
Q. Can I get English notes from a Korean lecture?
A. Yes. With the app in English, summaries, quizzes and answers are written in English while the transcript keeps the original language.
Q. Does it work with PDFs and slides?
A. Text-based PDFs and PPTX files are summarized page by page. Scanned image-only PDFs can't be read yet.
Q. Is it free?
A. The free plan includes 120 minutes a month. Audio and video use one minute per minute; documents use one minute per page.

AI summaries, questions and feedback can contain mistakes. Double-check important points against the original.
